Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register?

Create an account using the Register page by entering your name, email, phone number, role, and password.

How do I reset my password?

Go to the Forgot Password page and submit your email to receive reset instructions.

Can hospital staff use the system?

Yes. The system supports different roles such as patient, staff, and doctor.

Is this system secure?

Yes. The design supports secure login, role-based access, and protected user workflows.
